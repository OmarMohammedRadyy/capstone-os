# Capstone OS — Cinematic Landing Experience Specification

## 1. Creative Direction & Positioning
Capstone OS rejects generic SaaS, startup, neon-AI, and glassmorphic templates. Its public entry and narrative experience are built around five pillars:
- **Cinematic Editorial**
- **Quiet Luxury**
- **Academic Command Center**
- **Premium Productivity**
- **Physical 3D Storytelling**

The emotional register is **calm, weighted, precise, structured, luxurious, and intelligent**.

---

## 2. Core Visual Narrative
Every scroll progression on the landing page advances a single, uninterrupted story:

$$\text{Chaos} \longrightarrow \text{Structure} \longrightarrow \text{Progress} \longrightarrow \text{Control} \longrightarrow \text{Submission} \longrightarrow \text{Defense}$$

A graduation project typically begins as scattered research PDFs (`final.pdf`, `final2.pdf`, `final-really-final.pdf`), fragmented chat messages, unlinked meeting notes, and untracked supervisor comments. Capstone OS aligns every artifact into one connected academic operating system.

## 4. Brand Scale & Visual Usage Ratio
- **80% Neutral / Dark Surfaces:** Light (`#F5F3ED` Canvas, `#FAF9F5` Surface, `#FFFFFF` Elevated) / Dark (`#070A08` Canvas, `#0C110E` Surface, `#121913` Elevated).
- **15% Deep Forest Green:** `#07110A` (`Forest 950`) through `#477C55` (`Forest 500`).
- **5% Antique Gold & Warm Copper:** `#C9A13C` (`Gold 500`), `#B38627` (`Gold 600`), `#B86A39` (`Copper 500`). Gold is strictly reserved for fine strokes, active indicators, and editorial accents—never large bright yellow fills.
