# Capstone OS — Official AI Video & Image Production Prompts

## Production Rules
1. **Clean Cinematic Plates Only:** Never ask the video/image generator to render readable UI text, logos, or labels. All typography is composited by the Capstone OS frontend.
2. **Unified Color Grade:** Deep forest green (`#07110A` – `#214B30`), warm charcoal black (`#070A08`), antique gold (`#C9A13C`), muted copper (`#B86A39`), and warm ivory paper (`#F5F3ED`).
3. **Strict Rejections:** Reject any generation with purple/blue neon, holograms, sci-fi HUDs, malformed hands, synthetic faces, or unreadable fake typography.
